# Loan Default & Retention Analysis Dashboard

**Tool:** Power BI  
**Data:** Simulated loan portfolio dataset  
**Project Type:** Business Intelligence / Fintech Strategy

## Overview
This dashboard explores loan default rates and client retention trends across different industries using simulated fintech data. The goal was to simulate what a credit risk and growth opportunity dashboard might look like for decision-makers at a lending institution.

![Loan Default & Retention Analysis Dashboard](loan%20default%20and%20retention%20analysis%20dashboard.png)

## Key Metrics
- **Total Loan Amount:** $70.3M  
- **Overall Default Rate:** 25.4%  
- **Average Retention Rate:** 25.6%  
- **Default Rate by Industry:** Highlights risk concentration  
- **Retention Rate by Industry:** Highlights repeat customer opportunity  

## Business Insight
*Hospitality and Healthcare have higher-than-average default rates but also strong retention—suggesting these verticals may need better support, not just stricter credit terms.*

## Features
- Slicers for filtering by Region, Credit Band, Loan Amount, Industry, and Rep
- KPI Cards for total loans, default rate, and retention
- Stacked bar comparison of default vs. retention rate per industry

## Why I Built It
To simulate real-world portfolio risk analysis while highlighting areas of opportunity, not just risk. Built with hiring managers and fintech leaders in mind to demonstrate strategic thinking and storytelling through data.

> All data is simulated. No real client information is used.
